# Compact PRD

## Audience and problem

Who experiences the problem, and what observable limitation do they face?

## Desired outcomes

Describe the user-visible results. Separate outcomes from implementation choices.

## Scope

List included behavior, preservation requirements, constraints, and explicit non-goals.

## Acceptance criteria

Give each criterion a stable ID and an observable pass condition. Include relevant failure,
recovery, authorization, and compatibility cases.

## Decisions and open questions

Record material decisions, assumptions, unresolved choices, and the owner of each open question.
